# Pulse Support

## What is Pulse?

Pulse is a simple, vibrant interval timer for Apple Watch and iPhone — perfect for any timed workout with repetitions.
Set your exercise duration, rest period, and number of reps — then hit Start and let Pulse guide your training with haptic feedback and optional sound alerts.

---

## How to Use

### Setting Up a Session

1. Open the app and adjust your **Exercise** duration (5–300 seconds)
2. Set your **Rest** period (0–120 seconds)
3. Choose the number of **Repetitions** (1–50)
4. Tap **Start** — a 3-2-1 countdown begins before your session

### During a Session

1. The circular progress ring drains as each interval counts down
2. Haptic feedback signals each transition between exercise and rest
3. Tap **Pause** to pause, or **Stop** to end the session early

### Completion

When all repetitions are done, a completion screen shows your session summary. Tap **OK** to return to setup.

### Customizing Settings

- Open **Settings** (gear icon)
- Choose **vibration patterns** for interval start and end
- Set **exercise sounds** (start & end) — sounds only play during exercise, not rest
- Save your preferred **default values** for exercise, rest, and repetitions

---

## Key Features

- Exercise and rest interval timer with customizable repetitions
- 3-2-1 countdown before session starts
- Circular progress ring that drains in real time
- 3 haptic vibration patterns for start and end signals
- Optional sound alerts for exercise segments
- Works on Apple Watch (standalone — no iPhone required) and iPhone/iPad
- Supports light and dark mode on iOS
- Digital Crown support on Apple Watch

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I need an account?

No. Pulse requires **no account** — your settings are stored directly on your device.

### Is an internet connection required?

No. Pulse works **entirely offline** — no network connection is ever needed.

### Does Pulse track my location?

No. Pulse does not use or request location data.

### Does Pulse collect personal data?

No. Pulse is privacy-friendly:

- No personal accounts
- No data collection
- No tracking
- All data stays locally on your device

If you delete the app, your data is removed with it.
